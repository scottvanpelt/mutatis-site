Valuation, mutatis mutandis.

Most valuation scenarios change one input and hold everything else still. Mutatis changes them together: interest rates, inflation, growth, credit spreads and cash flows move the way an economy would, drawn from an ensemble of macroeconomic models.

Every valuation comes with its range, and with an account of where that range comes from.

What makes a Mutatis scenario different

The economy moves as a system

A rate shock carries through to inflation, growth and credit the way structural models say it should, so discount rates and cash flows stay consistent with each other.

Uncertainty, broken down by source

Each valuation range is split into what comes from disagreement between models, from parameters, from the company's sensitivity to the economy, and from the equity risk premium.

Tested without hindsight

Every backtest uses only the data that was published at the time, revisions included. Track records are earned, not fitted.
